Questions

Straight answers.

Does Lead Desk use AI?

We use AI to build and test your system faster: drafting message options, checking your setup and reviewing logs. The system itself follows fixed rules you've approved, because messages to your customers need to be predictable. AI-written replies may become an option later, but only with your approval and your own API key.

What do I need?

A Google account (Gmail or Google Workspace) that you send customer email from, and a website form or Google Form. If you use Outlook or Microsoft 365, tell us. A Microsoft version is planned but not available yet.

Are there monthly costs?

Not from us unless you choose Care. Lead Desk runs on Google's free automation service (Apps Script). Google limits how many emails it sends per day: 100 for free Gmail accounts and 1,500 for Workspace. That's plenty for most small businesses, and we check your volume before setup.

Who can see my customers' details?

Your customers' details stay in your Google account. During setup we work in your account with your permission, and we don't keep copies after handover.

What if something breaks?

Errors are logged and emailed to you, at most once an hour. The first 30 days of fixes are included. After that, Care covers fixes, or we quote per job.

Can Banira automate other things?

Yes. Lead Desk is our first standard system. We also take on custom work such as scheduling, reporting, CRM updates and email workflows, quoted after a call.
