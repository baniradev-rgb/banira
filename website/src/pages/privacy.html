Who we are

Banira is operated by {%owner.legal_name%}, {%owner.address%} (“Banira”, “we”). For anything about privacy, email {%owner.email%}.

What we collect, in short

  • Identifiers and contact details you give us: name, email, phone (optional), business name.
  • What you tell us in the contact form or during a project: how your business handles inquiries, your tools, hours and message wording.
  • Payment records from Stripe: amount, date, your name and email, billing address if Stripe asks for it, and the card brand and last four digits. Never the full card number.
  • Technical data our host records for security: IP address, browser type, pages requested and time.

We don’t collect sensitive information such as health, financial account or government ID numbers, and we ask you not to send it.

This website

  • No tracking cookies, no advertising and no third-party analytics. Fonts and scripts are served from our own site.
  • Settings in your browser: the pricing option you chose, for the current visit only, so the contact form can preselect it (sessionStorage); and your colour theme if you pick one (localStorage). Neither leaves your device.
  • Hosting: the site is hosted by Netlify, which keeps standard request logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to run and protect the service. See Netlify’s privacy policy.
  • Analytics: none. If we ever add privacy-friendly, cookie-free measurement, we’ll update this notice first.

The contact form

The form collects what you type: your name and email, and optionally your business name, phone, the email system and website tool you use, the option you’re interested in, and your message. Submissions are received by Netlify Forms, which filters spam (using Akismet), and are delivered to Banira’s business mailbox. We use them only to reply and, if you ask, to prepare a proposal.

Payments

Payments are made on Stripe’s checkout and billing portal. Stripe collects your card details directly and processes them under its own privacy policy; it may use them to prevent fraud. Banira receives the payment record described above, which we keep for accounting and tax.

Client projects

When we build for you, we work inside your own accounts (for Lead Desk: your Google Sheet, Gmail and Apps Script) by screen-share or with access you grant. We keep your intake answers and the configuration we generate (business name, hours, services, message templates) to deliver and support the project.

Your customers’ data (Lead Desk)

  • Inquiries from your customers are stored in your Google Sheet, and emails are sent from your mailbox. Banira does not copy them to its own systems.
  • For these records you are the business responsible for them (the “controller”). During setup and Care, Banira acts only on your instructions, and we can sign a short data-processing agreement if you need one.
  • The Lead Desk log records events and IDs, not the content of customers’ messages.
  • Your customers’ data is not used to train AI models.

How we use personal information

  • To reply to you and to plan, build and support what you buy.
  • To take payment, send receipts and keep business and tax records.
  • To keep the site and our accounts secure and to prevent spam and fraud.
  • To meet legal obligations.

For visitors in the UK or EU, our legal bases are: steps you ask for before a contract, performing the contract, our legitimate interest in replying to inquiries and running a secure business, and legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell personal information, and we don’t share it for cross-site advertising. We share it only with the service providers that run the business, under their terms:

  • Netlify: website hosting and the contact form.
  • Stripe: payments, receipts and the billing portal.
  • Google (Gmail and Google Workspace): Banira’s business email and documents.
  • Anthropic (Claude): we use AI tools to help prepare research, configurations and documents. Information about your business may be used with them to prepare your proposal. We don’t put your customers’ personal data into these tools.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, or to protect our rights in a dispute.

How long we keep it

  • Inquiries that don’t become projects: deleted within 12 months of our last contact.
  • Project intake files: deleted 30 days after handover, unless you take Care.
  • Payment records, invoices and business records: kept as long as tax law requires.

Your rights and choices

Anyone can ask us to see what we hold about them, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a purpose. Email {%owner.email%}. We’ll reply within 30 days and may need to confirm it’s you. Depending on where you live (for example California, other US states, the UK or the EU) you may have further rights, including to appeal our answer or complain to a regulator. We won’t treat you differently for using them.

We’re not required to keep some records forever, but we must keep payment and tax records for the period the law sets, even if you ask us to delete them.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

This site doesn’t track visitors across other websites and doesn’t allow third parties to do so, so there is nothing for a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal to switch off. Every visitor gets the same no-tracking experience.

Children

Banira’s services are for businesses. The site isn’t directed at children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this notice
