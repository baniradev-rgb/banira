Care ($49 USD a month)

  • Cancel any time, online in the Stripe billing portal (link on the pay page), or by email. No cancellation fee.
  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you’ve paid for, and you keep Care until then. We don’t refund part of a month.
  • If we charged you after you cancelled, or charged you twice, we refund it in full.
  • If we end Care, we refund any month paid but not provided.

How to ask for a refund

Email {%owner.email%} with your name and the date of the payment. We reply within 5 business days. Refunds go back to the card or method you paid with.

How long it takes

Once we issue a refund, Stripe sends it straight away. It usually takes 5–10 business days to appear on your statement, depending on your bank.

If you disagree

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank; most problems are quicker to fix directly. This policy doesn’t limit any right you have under the law.
