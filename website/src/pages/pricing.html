Pricing questions.

Why are prices provisional?

Banira is new and these prices haven’t been tested with clients yet. The price in your written plan is fixed once you approve it.

Do I get the dashboard shown on this site?

No. You receive the setup in your Google account: the sheet, the script, your messages, alerts and the daily summary. The interface on this site is a sample, and Workspace is a concept.

What if it doesn’t work with my setup?

We check compatibility in the first conversation, before you pay. Refund terms for problems before go-live are still being finalised in our draft terms.

Why is the founding price lower?

The first three clients help us improve Lead Desk with a feedback call. It’s a real limit of three, with no deadline.

Do I have to take Care?

No. Lead Desk runs without it, and the first 30 days of fixes are included either way.
