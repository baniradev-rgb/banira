An exploratory interface: one place to see inquiries, follow-ups and connected workflows. It is not an available product. Lead Desk today is delivered in your own Google Sheet and Gmail.

Why we’re exploring it

One view instead of several inboxes.

A small business that automates a few things ends up with scripts, sheets and emails in different places. Lead Desk already sends a daily summary. The question we’re exploring is whether owners would value one calm screen showing what needs them, what’s waiting, and what each workflow is doing.

Before any of this is built, we’d need to validate the problem with real clients, the permissions it would need, who would use it, how data would be stored, and what it would cost to run. Until then it stays a concept.
