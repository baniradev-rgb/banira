Payment security

Your card stays with Stripe.

  • The pay buttons open Stripe’s own checkout page on buy.stripe.com, over HTTPS. Banira’s site has no card fields and never receives card numbers.
  • Stripe is a PCI DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. Banira sees the payment, your name and email, and the last four digits of the card.
  • We never ask for card details, passwords or payment by email or phone.
