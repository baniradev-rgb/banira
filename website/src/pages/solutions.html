Who it’s for

Home-service businesses, first.

HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing and remodeling companies with a few to a few dozen staff, where the owner or one office person handles inquiries between jobs.

A good fit if

  • Inquiries arrive through a form on your website
  • You send customer email from Gmail or Google Workspace
  • Evening and weekend inquiries wait until someone is free
  • You’d like to know which inquiries to call first
  • You want to own the setup rather than rent software

Probably not a fit if

  • You already use an all-in-one platform with automatic replies
  • Your email is Microsoft 365 or Outlook only
  • Most inquiries arrive by phone or text, not a form
  • You get fewer than two web inquiries a week
  • You handle health, payment or other sensitive data in the form

Custom automation

Beyond Lead Desk, scoped one process at a time.

Some processes don’t fit a standard service: a weekly numbers email, filing form responses, keeping two records in step. We can scope these as custom projects.

  • Free first conversation to check the process is worth automating. Our readiness check helps.
  • A written plan with the steps, what stays manual and a fixed price, before any work starts.
  • Built in your accounts, so you own the result.

We haven’t delivered a custom project yet, so we don’t publish a starting price. Lead Desk is our focus.
