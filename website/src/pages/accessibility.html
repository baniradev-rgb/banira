Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this site, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, zoom or reduced motion.

Our target

This site aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. We haven’t had an independent audit, so we describe this as a target rather than a certified result.

What we test on every release

  • An automated check (axe-core) of every page, in the light and dark themes, at phone and desktop widths. Automated checks find only some problems, so we also test by hand.
  • Keyboard paths: the menu, the theme picker, the demo, the scenario composer and the contact form, including a skip link and visible focus.
  • Reduced motion: with your system set to reduce motion, the background animation stops and content appears without movement.
  • Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 in both themes, layouts checked from 360 px to 1920 px wide, and text content that stays readable without JavaScript.
  • Form errors are announced, listed at the top of the form and linked to the field.

Known limitations

  • We haven’t yet tested with screen readers on real iOS or Android devices.
  • The interactive demo and the sample product screens are dense. They work by keyboard, but they’re easier to follow visually; the same information is given in plain text on the Lead Desk and How it works pages.
  • The moving background on the Lead Desk page is decorative and hidden from screen readers.
  • Stripe’s checkout and billing portal are run by Stripe and covered by Stripe’s own accessibility work, not ours.
