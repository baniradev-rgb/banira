Data flow, access and boundaries.

Lead Desk runs in your Google account and sends email as you, so it has to be careful. Here is where data goes, what access it needs, the safeguards it has, and what we have and haven’t verified.

Data flow

Inquiries stay in your account.

A form submission goes to the script attached to your sheet, is written to that sheet, and replies go out from your Gmail. Banira’s systems are not in that path.

Stored

In your Google Sheet: name, email, phone, ZIP, message, score, status and follow-up history. A log sheet records events and IDs, not message contents.

Sent

From your Gmail: the approved replies and follow-ups to customers, and alerts and summaries to you. Error emails contain no customer details.

Not kept by Banira

We don’t copy inquiries to our systems. Intake answers and your configuration are kept to deliver the work, and deleted 30 days after handover unless you choose Care.

Google asks you to approve these when the script is first set up. You can review or remove them in your Google account at any time.

Safeguards

Designed so a mistake stays small.

  • Send limits. One automatic reply per address per 24 hours, plus hourly and daily caps, so the form can’t be used to flood an inbox.
  • Fixed messages. Replies come from approved templates. The visitor’s text is never echoed; their first name is used only after links, symbols and formulas are removed.
  • Quota reserve. Automatic replies stop before the daily email limit so your alerts still get through.
  • Bot filters. A hidden honeypot field, a minimum time to fill the form, link-spam scoring, and an optional bot check verified server-side.
  • Duplicate protection. The same person sending the same message twice within a day makes one record and one reply.
  • Spreadsheet-safe text. Text starting with =, +, - or @ is stored as plain text, not run as a formula.
  • No double follow-ups. A lock prevents overlapping runs, and each follow-up is recorded before and after sending. An interrupted send is flagged for a person to review, not retried automatically.
  • Honest test mode. Records what would be sent, sends nothing, and doesn’t use up follow-ups.
  • Failures are visible. If an email fails, the inquiry is still recorded, marked needs attention, and reported to you.

Boundaries

What Lead Desk can’t protect against.

Google availability

It depends on Google’s services and quotas. If they’re down, sending waits.

Inbox placement

We can’t guarantee every email reaches the inbox. Domain authentication helps.

Your account security

It’s only as secure as your Google account. Use two-step verification.

Sensitive data

Not designed for health, payment or ID data. Ask customers not to send it through the form.

Verification status
