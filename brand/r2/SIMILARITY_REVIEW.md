## Mark: curved pod + separate dot
The mark is an abstract stroke-and-dot form. Families it could be compared with:
- **Slash-and-dot marks** (for example Slashdot's "/." identity). R2's pod is a curved, tapered, filled organic shape, not a straight slash, and the dot sits lower right at about 60 % of the height. The overall impression differs, but the genre is shared. [logokit: Slashdot](https://logokit.com/brands/slashdot.org)
- **Comma / apostrophe and dot marks**, and the "i" glyph rotated. These are common abstract shapes, so distinctiveness comes from the specific pod curvature and spacing, which is why the V4 refit kept the approved geometry exactly (`FIT_REPORT.md`).
- **Leaf + dot or seed marks** in agriculture and wellness. These are a different field, but a visual search in classes 9, 35 and 42 should include "leaf" and "seed" design codes (Vienna classification 05.03 leaves, 26.01 circles).
- Searches for distinctive slash-and-dot technology logos did not surface an identical mark ([designrush: Stripe](https://www.designrush.com/best-design/stripe) and [StarTech brand guide](https://sgcdn.startech.com/005329/media/pages/partners/startech-brand-guide-logo.pdf) use slash and dot elements in wordmarks, not as a standalone pod and seed).
