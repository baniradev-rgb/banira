Home/How it works

From first conversation to a setup you own.

Six stages, about a week from your approval, and roughly 90 minutes of your time in total. You approve every message before anything goes live.

The stages

What happens, in order.

The timings are our plan for each installation. We’ll confirm them with you in the written plan.

  1. Discovery

    A short call about how inquiries reach you today: your form, your inbox, who answers, what gets missed. We tell you plainly if Lead Desk isn’t a fit.

    You: 15–20 minutes · Banira: an honest assessment · Free

  2. Mapping

    We map your services, hours, service area and urgent words, and agree what counts as a high-value inquiry. You answer about ten questions.

    Output: your configuration, ready to check

  3. Approval

    A written plan with the fixed price, and every message your customers could receive shown with sample customers. Nothing is built until you approve it.

    You: read, edit, reply “approved”

  4. Configuration

    On a screen-share in your Google account, we create the tracker sheet, attach the Lead Desk script with your settings, and connect your website form or a Google Form.

    You: 60–90 minutes · we never need your password

  5. Testing

    First in test mode, which records what would be sent and sends nothing. Then one live test with email addresses you control: reply, alert, reply detection and “stop”.

    Checks: tiers, templates, timing, error alerts, opt-out

  6. Handover

    We show you how to read the sheet, mark leads booked or won, and pause everything in about ten seconds. You get a handover note, and we remove our access unless you choose Care.

    Then: day-7 check-in, 30 days of fixes

Who does what

Clear responsibilities.

You

  • Describe how inquiries reach you
  • Approve the messages, rules and price
  • Grant access in your Google account during setup
  • Send one live test from an address you control
  • Tell us if anything looks wrong

Banira

  • Write the plan, messages and configuration
  • Install and test in your account
  • Document how it works and how to pause it
  • Remove access at handover
  • Fix problems for 30 days after go-live

How we use AI

AI helps us build. People decide.

Banira uses AI tools to research, draft and test faster, which keeps setup prices modest. What runs in your account is rules and templates, not AI.

  • AI may draft message options. You choose and approve the final words.
  • AI helps write and check code. Automated tests and a person review it.
  • Your customers’ data stays in your account and isn’t put into AI tools.
