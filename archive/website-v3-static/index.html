Inquiry handling

Every inquiry gets a considered first reply.

When someone fills in your website form, Lead Desk saves it to your sheet and sends the reply you approved for that situation: office hours, after hours, or urgent. Then it scores the inquiry so you can see who matters most.

Follow-up and visibility

Polite follow-up, and a clear view of what needs you.

If a customer doesn’t answer, Lead Desk follows up in the same email thread on days 1, 3 and 7, then stops. You get an alert for each new inquiry and a summary every morning at 7:00.

A reply check runs every hour. When the customer answers, the next check stops the remaining follow-ups and the summary tells you to reply personally.

Automations at work

Three routines run in your account every day: replying to each inquiry, checking for answers every hour, and summarising the morning. Each one only does what you approved.

About Care
Do I get the dashboard shown above?

No. The sample interface explains how Lead Desk works. What you receive is the documented setup in your own Google account: a Google Sheet tracker, the script, your approved Gmail messages, alerts and a daily summary email. The Workspace view is a concept.

What do I need to have already?

A Google account you send customer email from (free Gmail works, with Google’s limit of about 100 automated recipients a day; Workspace allows more) and a website form, or a Google Form we set up.

Who owns it?

You do. It runs in your account, the data stays there, and it keeps working if we stop working together. We remove our access at handover unless you choose Care.

Is Lead Desk available now?

We’re taking first conversations. Lead Desk has passed its automated tests against simulated Google services; each installation is verified live in your account, with test addresses you control, before it goes live.

Lead Desk · rule

Approved replies

Every message a customer can receive is written with you and approved before go-live. Lead Desk picks one of three acknowledgements by time and urgency, fills in the customer's first name (cleaned) and your details, and never repeats what the visitor typed.

Lead Desk · rule

Priority rules

Each inquiry gets a score from 0 to 100 with the reasons attached, so you can see why it matters. Scores of 60 or more are hot, 30–59 are warm, and lower scores are cold. Spam is filtered first and never gets a reply.

Default weights (adjusted during setup)

Urgent wording (e.g. “no heat”, “today”)+25

Urgent words in the sample: no heat, emergency, gas smell, smell gas, carbon monoxide, water leaking, freezing, asap, urgent, today.

Current limits

  • Rules match words and fields. They don't understand meaning the way a person does, so unusual wording can be scored low.
  • Scoring is a guide for who to call first. It never decides whether someone gets a reply.
  • Service-area checks use ZIP code prefixes.

Lead Desk · rule

Follow-up schedule

If the customer hasn't answered, Lead Desk sends up to three short follow-ups in the same email thread, on days 1, 3, 7 after the inquiry (hot and warm: 3, cold: 1). They stop when the customer replies, asks to stop, or you change the status in your sheet.

  • Reply check: runs every hour, so a follow-up stops at the next check after the customer answers, not instantly.
  • Out-of-office replies don’t count as an answer, so follow-ups continue.
  • Pause one lead: set its status to booked, won, lost or manual and no further follow-ups are sent.
  • Pause everything: switch test mode on; nothing is sent until you switch it off.

Current limits

  • Follow-ups are sent by an hourly run, so they can go out up to an hour after they are due.
  • A reply has to come from the customer's address in the same thread to be recognised.
  • If a send is interrupted, the lead is flagged for review instead of being retried automatically.

Lead Desk · rule

Owner alerts

You get an email for each new inquiry with its score, the reasons and the customer's details, plus a summary every morning at 7:00 of new leads, customers who replied and anything that needs your attention.

  • Needs attention: if a reply can’t be sent, the inquiry is still saved, marked, and included in your alert and summary.
  • Errors are written to a log sheet and emailed to you at most once an hour, without customer details.

Current limits

  • Alerts are email only. Push notifications or texts are not offered.
  • Alerts are capped per hour; anything over the cap is covered by the daily summary.
  • The sample alert layout is illustrative; the content matches what the engine produces.
