# BRAND_GUIDELINES — v3 (R2)

## Idea
**Banira** (バニラ) is how "vanilla" sounds in Japanese: the plain, dependable base that other flavours are built on. Banira builds the dependable systems a small business runs on, and builds them precisely. The philosophy: sophisticated technology should feel effortless to use.
Personality: simple, precise, intelligent, adaptable, reliable, clear, quietly confident.

## Logo: R2
The mark is a gently curved vanilla pod with a separate seed (dot) to its right. Source and kit are in `brand/r2/`; see `FIT_REPORT.md` for vector precision and `SIMILARITY_REVIEW.md` for the preliminary name and mark review (not a trademark clearance).
| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `svg/banira-r2-mark-{black,white}.svg` | Mark, 24 px and up |
| `svg/banira-r2-mark-small-{black,white}.svg` | Optical small-size variant (fuller pod, larger seed), 16–24 px |
| `svg/banira-r2-lockup-{black,white}.svg` | Mark + wordmark (primary) |
| `svg/banira-r2-wordmark-{black,white}.svg` | Text-only |
| `svg/banira-r2-app-{dark,light}.svg`, `png/` | Avatars, app tiles |
| `svg/favicon.svg` | Favicon; swaps black/white with the system theme |
| `svg/banira-r2-mark-web.svg` | Website component (`currentColor`, `--seed`) |
**Rules:** pure black on light, pure white on dark. No beige, gold, gradients, outlines, shadows or rotation. Clear space equals the seed diameter on all sides. Don't separate, re-space or recolour the seed. The single exception is inside product UI, where the seed may show a live status colour. The wordmark is lowercase "banira" in Geist 560 at −0.03 em, set beside the mark.

## Colour, type, motion
Monochrome foundation with designed dark (default) and light themes. Meaning colours appear only inside product UI. Geist and Geist Mono (SIL OFL), self-hosted. Details: DESIGN_SYSTEM.md, DESIGN_TOKENS.md, MOTION_SYSTEM.md.

## Voice
Plain, specific, calm. Say what the system does, what it costs and what it can't do. Never: "revolutionary", "AI-powered everything", "10x", "seamless", "set and forget". Quote numbers only with a source; label estimates, samples and provisional prices.
- ✔ "Every inquiry gets a considered first reply, even at 11 pm."
- ✘ "Supercharge your growth with cutting-edge AI agents!"

## Japanese
バニラ appears as a quiet secondary detail (footer, About, Open Graph image), never as decoration on its own.
